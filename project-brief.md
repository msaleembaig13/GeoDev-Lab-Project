# My Project Brief

## The question

Which wards in Dera Ghazi Khan Local Government Area, Punjab, are more than 7km from a health site?
## Why it matters

Dera Ghazi Khan includes both urban and rural settlements, with access to health facilities varying across different wards. Identifying wards where settlements are more than 7 km by road from the nearest health site can highlight areas with potential healthcare accessibility gaps. This ward-level analysis could help the local health department, NGOs, and development planners identify where additional primary healthcare facilities, mobile clinics, or outreach services may be needed.

## The Data I Need

- Ward boundaries   —  Dera Ghazi LGA, Punjab 
- Health facility locations   —  Punjab, Points
- Roads  —  Dera Ghazi LGA,

   ## What I would build

   A map of **Dera Ghazi Khan District** identifying wards and settlements more than **7 km by road from the nearest health facility**.
The map will highlight potential **healthcare accessibility gaps** and underserved areas.
Longer term, it could become a **healthcare accessibility dashboard** for health departments.
